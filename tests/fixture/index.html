Pet insurance for Australian dogs and cats

Pet insurance helps you cover the cost of veterinary treatment when your dog or cat gets sick or injured. Policies in Australia generally fall into two groups: accident-only cover, which handles injuries and poisoning, and comprehensive cover, which adds illness, diagnostics and surgery.

Most owners start by comparing the reimbursement rate, the annual benefit limit and the excess. A policy that looks cheap at the quote stage can end up costing more once you claim, especially if the reimbursement rate sits at sixty per cent.

Where to start

If your pet is young and healthy, a comprehensive policy with a high annual limit usually gives the best long term value. If your pet is older, or you simply want cover for the unexpected, accident-only cover can be a sensible middle ground.

Owners often ask whether routine care is worth adding. It covers vaccinations, desexing, dental cleaning and annual health checks, but the annual sub-limit is usually lower than the cost of the add-on itself.

Before you buy, check the waiting periods, the exclusion list and whether your preferred vet is covered by the insurer's network. Breed-specific exclusions are common for dogs prone to hip dysplasia or skin conditions.
